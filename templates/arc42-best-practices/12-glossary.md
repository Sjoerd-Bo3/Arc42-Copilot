# 12. Glossary

> **Purpose**: Create a shared language. Misunderstood terms cause expensive bugs and wasted meetings. Define every term that could be ambiguous.

> **Tip**: Include domain terms that developers might not know AND technical terms that business stakeholders might not know.

> **Tip**: If your team spans languages (e.g., English docs, Dutch domain), add a translations table.

> **Anti-pattern**: Glossary with only obvious terms. "API: Application Programming Interface" adds no value. "Work Order: the operational job in Pitstop (not the planning appointment)" prevents real confusion.
